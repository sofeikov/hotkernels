## 1. The loop `make_kernel.py` writes for the shape

- **Idea:** `add(V0,mul(max(S0,mul(V1,V2)),mul(V3,V4)))` as 5 operations on two ymm registers of four per iteration, each rounding once, in the order of NumPy's passes; every load of an iteration before its store, every address the array's base plus one index. A remainder of 1 to 7 elements is one more block that ends exactly at the end of the arrays, and fewer than 8 elements are done one at a time. Written by `prototypes/lazy/make_kernel.py`, not by hand.
