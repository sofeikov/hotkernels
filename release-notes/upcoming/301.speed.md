Elementwise operations on small arrays, such as `a + b`, `a * 2.0`, `a > 0`, `np.exp(a)` and `x += y`, on bool, integer, `float32` and `float64` arrays of up to 16,384 elements, now skip most of NumPy's call path and run NumPy's own loop, with the same results and warnings. At 16 `float64` elements `a + b` is about 2x faster and `a > 0` about 3x, measured on an Apple M1 Pro.
