`x[mask] = v` on `float32`, `int32` and `int64` (and `np.putmask`, `np.copyto(x, v, where=mask)`) is served by a kernel when `v` is a plain number of the array's kind, and `x[mask]`, `np.compress` and `np.extract` on `int32` too: on x86-64, `x[mask] = 0.0` on a million `float32` with half the mask True is about 31x faster than NumPy, and 23x at 4,096 elements.
