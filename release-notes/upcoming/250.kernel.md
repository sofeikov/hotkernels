`np.sum(x)` and `x.sum()` on a one-dimensional `float32` array are served by a kernel on Apple Silicon, with NumPy's result bit for bit: from Python on an Apple M1 Pro, about 20x faster than NumPy at 16 elements, 10x at 4,096 and 2x at a million. It is installed by default; other arguments, other shapes, and sums that overflow or meet a NaN or an infinity stay with NumPy.
