`np.unique(x)` on `int64` labels is faster on Linux x86-64: about 1.3x at a million elements and 1.8x at 4,096, on an Intel Core i5-14600KF, which is about 39x faster than NumPy at a million. The gain is for labels that keep to 64 neighbouring values or come sorted; labels spread evenly over a wider range run about as before.
