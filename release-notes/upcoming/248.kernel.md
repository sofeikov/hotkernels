`m.any(axis=0)` and `np.any(m, axis=0)` on a two-dimensional boolean array in C order are served by a kernel. On x86-64 (Intel Core i5-14600KF) a mask of 30 rows by 31 columns is about 9x faster than NumPy, and a matrix of 31 columns about 18x at 4,096 elements and 37x at a million. Calls with `keepdims`, `out` or `where` go to NumPy as before.
