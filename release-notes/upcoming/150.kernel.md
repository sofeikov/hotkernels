`np.maximum.accumulate` on `int64`, and `np.fmax.accumulate` on `int64` and `float64`, are served by kernels for one-dimensional arrays: measured on x86-64, about 5.3x and 5x faster than NumPy at a million elements and 2.6x and 2.8x at 16. `np.maximum.accumulate` on floats, which carries NaN forward, stays with NumPy, and so does a `float64` array holding a signalling NaN, a negative zero, or NaNs of different bit patterns. On ARM64 the same calls run a plain C loop for now.
